About Me

I am a Research Engineer specializing in computer vision, XR (VR/AR/MR), and real-time AI systems, with a focus on healthcare and human motion analysis.

I hold a Master’s degree in Computer Science from Politecnico di Torino, where my thesis was published at IEEE COMPSAC 2023. My work focuses on building applied systems that combine machine learning, computer vision, and XR technologies for rehabilitation, robotics, and intelligent environments.

Previously, I worked as a Robotics Engineer at the Institute of Intelligent Equipment in Suzhou, developing robotic systems involving SLAM, perception, and human–robot interaction. I am currently open to opportunities in XR, computer vision, AI engineering, and related PhD positions in Switzerland and across Europe.
